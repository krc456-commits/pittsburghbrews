# Brewery Profile Batch 1

Status: research started 2026-09-19. Do not publish until the Hitchhiker profile format is approved.

## 1. Dancing Gnome
- Brand-level profile with both 1025 Main taproom and 925 Main HQ.
- Strong angle: founder Brett Herron's hop obsession, 2016 opening, hop-forward "American Ale" identity, Sharpsburg growth.
- Primary sources:
  - https://dancinggnomebeer.com/our-story/
  - https://dancinggnomebeer.com/

## 2. Old Thunder Brewing
- Single Blawnox brewery profile.
- Strong angle: former 1939 Blawnox Post Office converted into the taproom; lager/ale precision plus wood-aged mixed fermentation.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.oldthunderbrewing.com/about-1
  - https://www.oldthunderbrewing.com/post/the-announcement

## 3. East End Brewing Company
- One profile covering Larimer Brewpub, Mt. Lebanon Taproom, and current North Side pop-up if still active when drafted.
- Strong angle: Pittsburgh brewery since 2004; founder Scott Smith; Big Hop; 90-neighborhood "You Are Here" beer project; pizza program.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.eastendbrewing.com/
  - https://www.eastendbrewing.com/about-our-beers
  - https://www.eastendbrewing.com/you-are-here-beer-neighborhood-beers

## 4. Penn Brewery
- One profile covering North Side brewery/restaurant plus airport presence as a secondary location.
- Strong angle: modern brewery launched in 1986, historic Eberhardt & Ober site with brewing roots to 1848, surviving lagering caves, German beer and Pittsburgh-German food.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.pennbrew.com/our-story-1
  - https://www.pennbrew.com/

## 5. Church Brew Works
- Single Lawrenceville brewpub profile.
- Strong angle: brewery and restaurant inside a restored historic church; architecture is the central visitor experience.
- Primary source:
  - https://churchbrew.com/
- Additional historical source still needed before drafting founder/building timeline.

## 6. Grist House
- One brand profile covering Millvale, Command/Oakdale, Beer Crib/Butler, and Beer Market/Saxonburg.
- Strong angle: community/family identity, dog-friendly culture, wide beer range, and dramatically different taproom formats.
- Primary sources:
  - https://gristhouse.com/
  - https://gristhouse.com/locations/
  - https://gristhouse.com/beer/

## 7. Brew Gentlemen
- One Braddock brand profile; note current private-event taproom status and Braddock Public House pickup.
- Strong angle: two college friends, Braddock steel-town setting beside Edgar Thomson Works, soft/balanced brewing identity, General Braddock's IPA and local-history branding.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.brewgentlemen.com/pages/about
  - https://www.brewgentlemen.com/

## 8. Cinderlands Beer Company
- One brand profile covering Warehouse, Wexford Taproom, and Long Story Short.
- Strong angle: founded by Jamie & Joanna Warden and Paul & Emily Schneider; original 2017 Lawrenceville brewery; 17,000+ sq. ft. Warehouse expansion in 2019; beer + serious food program.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.cinderlands.com/cinderlands-story/
  - https://www.cinderlands.com/
  - https://www.cinderlands.com/cinderlands-warehouse/

## 9. Allegheny City Brewing
- One profile covering East Ohio Street and Brighton Heights.
- Strong angle: founders Al, Amy and Matt returned from Colorado; opened in 2016; brewery name deliberately ties to the former City of Allegheny/North Side history; neighborhood-first identity.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.alleghenycitybrewing.com/our-story
  - https://www.alleghenycitybrewing.com/

## 10. Abjuration Brewing
- One brand profile covering McKees Rocks/Parkway Theater, Hazelwood Brewhouse, and Mt. Lebanon TAG taproom presence if still current at publication.
- Strong angle: founders Dave Hallam and Tom Glover; experimental brewing, version-numbered beers and unusual ingredients; original brewery built into the Parkway Theater; now operating across very different spaces.
- Primary sources:
  - https://www.abjurationbrewing.com/
  - https://breweriesinpa.com/meet-the-brewer-dave-hallam-of-abjuration-brewing/
- Hazelwood building history:
  - https://hazelwoodbrewhouse.com/history/

## Batch rule
One brewery brand = one canonical profile page. Multiple physical taprooms/branches are described inside that page and remain separate location records for directory/search/crawl purposes.
